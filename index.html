How it works

A simple reset, one day at a time.

Twenty-one days gives you a clear place to start, with enough flexibility for your actual schedule.

1

Choose your focus

Pick a few habits that feel useful for your current season—like hydration, movement, sleep, or study breaks.

2

Check in each day

Use your guide and tracker to complete a small action, notice how it felt, and keep your momentum visible.

3

Keep what works

Reflect during bi-weekly check-ins, celebrate your progress, and carry the routines that fit into your life.

✦ There is no perfect reset.
